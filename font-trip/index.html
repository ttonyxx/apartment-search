The birthday reservations stay fixed. Everything else moves with the weather. Climb while the rock is fully dry; when it isn’t, there is a château, a spa, a market, and a bottle of wine waiting.

Leave it better

The forest
has rules.

Sandstone remembers every careless visit. These are not suggestions; they are how the climbing survives.

Read the local ethics ↗
01

Dry means dry.

If the rock is damp, dark, cold-wet under an overhang, or wet on top, walk away. Chalk and towels do not make wet sandstone safe.

02

Clean feet, soft brush.

Use a carpet square before every attempt. Sand polishes footholds. Brush gently, use chalk sparingly, and erase tick marks.

03

Carry, don’t drag.

Keep pads off roots and vegetation, use existing paths, take every scrap of tape home, and leave engraved boulders untouched.

04

Leave before dark.

No night climbing or forest sleeping. Car parks close 10 PM—6 AM. Headlamps are for walking out, not another burn.
