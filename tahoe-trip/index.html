The Plan

Three days, dialed.

A lakeside warm-up, a big Donner power day stacked with the 4th of July, and a final send-or-float-home morning. Climb the best and the hardest — with cold water and fireworks built in.

01
July 3
Friday

Drive up & lakeside warm-up

Depart SFO 9:30 AM · ~213 mi up · West Shore granite
9:30 AM
Roll out of SFO — I-80 East over Donner Summit. ~213 mi; plan 4–5 hr (July-4 weekend traffic is real — leave on time).
Midday
Check into William Kent Campground (Hwy 89, ~2 mi south of Tahoe City). Pitch the tent in the pines, stash food in the bear locker.
PM
Drive ~20–30 min south to the Bliss / Meeks Bay lakeside boulders. Warm up on granite by the water: The Brain (V0), Big Face (V2), Bliss Arete (V4), Gunslinger (V5) — then touch a harder one, The Friendly Whale (V7).
Dusk
Flush the forearms with a cold lake swim, campfire dinner, early night. Tomorrow is the big one.

July 4
Saturday · 🎆

Donner power day → boat → fireworks

The marquee. Send V8, float the lake, light up the night.
Dawn
Drive ~35 min to Donner Summit / Donner Pass before the heat and holiday crowds. Cool granite = sticky friction.
AM
Ladder up — Masterwork (V4), Triple Crown (V6), Sweet Spot (V6) — then commit to the V8 trifecta: White Lines, Maximum Overdrive, Midnight Train. Reach higher on Hobo (V9). Pilgrimage: stand under The Outer Limits (V15) on the Flying Saucer (same boulder as Maximum Overdrive!).
PM
Back to camp, then rent the pontoon at Sunnyside Marina (right next door). Cruise the West Shore, anchor in a cove, swim in gin-clear water. Off the water by 6.
Night
Dinner, then post up for the 78th-annual Tahoe City fireworks — barge off Commons Beach, 9:30 PM. Watch from the beach or the William Kent shoreline.

Send Strategy

How to actually climb V8 this weekend.

Alpine granite at 7,000 ft rewards patience and cold fingers. A few rules to stack the odds.

🌅

Climb cool, climb early

Donner sits high — go at dawn for sticky friction. Midday sun greases the granite and your skin. Chase shade and a breeze.

🪜

Build the warm-up ladder

Never jump to V8 cold. Run V2 → V4 → V6 first. The trifecta wants warm tendons, fresh skin, and a fired-up nervous system.

🩹

Ration your skin

Hard granite shreds tips. Alternate burns with real rest, file flappers, tape early, and pack extra chalk for humid lakeside air.

🧗

Pads & spotters

Tahoe highballs are no joke. Bring 2+ crash pads, spot each other, and scope the landing before you commit up high.

💧

Altitude + hydration

6,000–7,000 ft plus alpine sun saps you fast. Water, electrolytes, sunscreen, and a hat — recovery is a performance multiplier.

🌿

Leave no trace

Pack out tape and tick, brush the holds clean, respect closures and wildlife, and keep all food in the bear lockers.
